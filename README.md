# OcreDex: Dofus Tracker
A little one-page tracker to make the Eternal Harvest quest easier to follow, one step at a time, all the way to the Ochre Dofus.

The goal is simple: spend less time keeping notes and more time playing.

What you can do:
- Track your progress: mark monsters as captured and see your overall completion, along with progress for each category and questv step.
- Find what you need: search the monster list and narrow it down with filters for type, quest step, or zone.
- Focus on what's left: hide monsters you've already captured when you want a cleaner to-do list.
- Keep separate character profiles: track progress for multiple characters, give them names, and switch between them.
- Save your progress automatically: your data is stored in your browser, so you can close the page and come back later on the same browser.
- Back up or move your progress: export your tracker data to a file and import it again when needed.
- Use your preferred language: switch between French, English, and Spanish.
- Keep an eye on each step: the step overview and progress bars help you see where you are in the quest.

## Usage

No installation is needed! It's a small browser-based tool. Access the website here:

https://ryswick.github.io/eternelle-moisson/

## Saving
Progress is saved in the browser you're using. It won't automatically follow you to another browser or device. Use the Export option to make a backup, and Import to restore it later.

If you clear your browser's site data, locally saved progress may be lost, so keeping an occasional backup is a good idea.

## Languages

The website is currently available in **French**, **English** and **Spanish**.
